Privacy policy

UltraIterator and UltraIterator Demo · last updated 27 October 2026

In short: UltraIterator doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy covers both apps, UltraIterator and UltraIterator Demo, made by Dan Wills.

Audio

The fractals move to sound, so the app can listen to your microphone, or to the audio your phone is playing (Android shows its own consent prompt for that). The sound is turned into a few numbers, how loud each pitch range is, many times a second, on your phone. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the app stops listening when you leave it.

Your music (UltraIterator only)

The full app can play music from a folder you choose with Android's folder picker. It only reads the files there in order to play them, on your phone.

Your pictures

The apps can show your own pictures as a slideshow. They only ever see the pictures you choose in Android's photo picker, or, in UltraIterator, the one folder you choose with Android's folder picker; they don't ask for permission to read your photos or storage. The pictures are read and shown on your phone, and never uploaded, copied or sent anywhere. The app remembers which pictures you chose and their shapes so it can show them again; "Forget Pictures" clears that list and gives back its access to them.

Settings

Your settings and presets are saved on your phone. If you export them, they go to a file you choose, and importing reads a file you choose. Nothing about them leaves your phone unless you share that file yourself.

The fractal images

The images are downloaded from a server run on Cloudflare and kept in the app's cache on your phone. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic request details in order to deliver the images, and may keep them briefly in its logs for security and reliability; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. I don't use this to identify or track anyone.

Children

The apps aren't aimed at children, and they don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
