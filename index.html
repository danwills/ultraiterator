UltraIterator

An Android slideshow of Ultra Fractal artwork by Dan Wills, moving to music.

About two thousand fractals, made over many years, drift past and dive into each other's zooms, in time with whatever's playing around you.

It comes in two editions: UltraIterator, and UltraIterator Demo, a free taste with a hand-picked selection.
